Novikov-Magnus framework yields time-local master equations for driven qubits under colored noise

A preprint combines two established techniques to systematically derive quantum master equations for systems driven under temporally correlated classical noise.

November 6, 2026

An unreviewed preprint posted to arXiv introduces a modular framework for constructing time-local quantum master equations governing driven quantum systems subject to classically correlated noise. The approach splices the Donsker-Furutsu-Novikov (DFN) functional response relation—which closes averages over stochastic trajectories—with the Magnus expansion, which organizes the time-ordered, noncommuting influence of noise perturbations along a controlled quantum trajectory. The authors call the resulting formalism the Novikov-Magnus master equation.

For zero-mean Gaussian noise, the framework is worked out explicitly to fourth order in the noise strength. The paper also sketches a natural extension to non-Gaussian noise, in which connected higher-order cumulants of the noise process weight correspondingly higher functional derivatives of the stochastic map. Applied to a driven qubit under two practically relevant noise models—Ornstein-Uhlenbeck and band-limited 1/f noise—the authors report that the second- and fourth-order maps remain completely positive and trace-preserving (CPTP) throughout the perturbative regime and reproduce stochastic simulations accurately.

Benchmarked against the cumulant-based pseudo-Lindblad master equation via the diamond norm, the Novikov-Magnus maps perform comparably at second order, with a modest accuracy gain at fourth order for Ornstein-Uhlenbeck noise. Because this work has not yet undergone peer review, the claimed advantages in structure preservation and perturbative accuracy should be treated as preliminary pending independent scrutiny.
