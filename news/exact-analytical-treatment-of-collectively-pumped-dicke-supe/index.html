Exact analytical solution derived for collectively pumped Dicke superradiance

A finite continued-fraction formalism exactly solves Dicke-ladder population dynamics under competing collective pump and decay, an unreviewed preprint claims.

October 27, 2026

An arXiv preprint (not yet peer-reviewed) reports an exact analytical treatment of Dicke superradiance in an ensemble of N two-level emitters subject simultaneously to collective decay at rate Γ and collective incoherent pumping at rate R. The system's population dynamics are confined to the fully symmetric Dicke ladder, but the quantum-trajectory picture that underlies them involves an infinite sum over competing pump-and-decay histories — a resummation problem the authors claim to have solved exactly.

The central result is a closed-form, finite continued fraction for the Laplace-transformed Dicke-state populations, obtained by resumming those infinitely many trajectories. The authors argue this construction gives a physically transparent interpretation of the tridiagonal resolvent structure and generalises to arbitrary permutationally symmetric initial states. They additionally extract perturbative transient solutions in the strong- and weak-pumping limits, and identify exact relaxation eigenvalues and emission observables at the balanced point Γ = R.

If the analysis withstands peer review, the continued-fraction framework could offer a systematic starting point for extending trajectory-based analytical methods to broader classes of driven-dissipative collective quantum systems. The result is presented as a preprint and has not yet been independently validated.
