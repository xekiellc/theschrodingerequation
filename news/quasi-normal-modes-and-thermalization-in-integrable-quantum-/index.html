TBA equations proposed to capture thermalization dynamics in integrable QFTs

A preprint links quasi-normal mode frequencies to Thermodynamic Bethe Ansatz, aiming to describe how integrable systems relax to equilibrium.

November 4, 2026

An unreviewed preprint posted to arXiv proposes a Thermodynamic Bethe Ansatz (TBA) framework for characterizing thermalization in integrable quantum field theories. The central claim is that the exponential damping and oscillatory behavior seen as a system approaches thermal equilibrium can be cast as analogs of quasi-normal mode (QNM) frequencies—quantities more familiar from the study of black hole perturbations in holography. The authors argue that holographic intuition, combined with known analytical properties of thermal Green's functions, motivates both the existence of such a TBA formulation and the imposition of a non-standard analytic structure on the resulting equations.

The paper conjectures that the proposed QNM TBA is equivalent to an all-order resummation of secular terms appearing in thermal form-factor expansions of retarded two-point correlation functions. To constrain the remaining free ingredients of the construction, the authors match against integrable form-factor expansions. They test their predictions against tensor network simulations of the E8 deformation of the Ising CFT at nonzero temperature, finding agreement that they present as supporting evidence for the framework.

The authors further conjecture that the QNM TBA approach could extend to out-of-equilibrium settings described by Generalized Hydrodynamics. Because this work has not yet undergone peer review, its claims—particularly the conjectured equivalence with secular-term resummation and the proposed GHD applicability—should be treated as promising but unverified hypotheses pending independent scrutiny.
